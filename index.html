Questions

Is TABO a real working prototype?+

It’s a real project in active prototyping. The mechanical design is done and we’re building and testing the electronics and software now. It isn’t shipping yet, and you’ll see real footage first in the Insider Group as each part starts working.

Does it require a monthly subscription?+

No. TABO is designed to work out of the box for $99. You can bring your own AI key to keep it free, or choose an optional cloud plan later for unlimited voice.

Is the camera recording me?+

No. TABO doesn’t record or upload video. Presence detection is designed to run on the device, and a still photo is only taken when you ask TABO to look at something. A physical switch also cuts power to the microphone.
